Solar Offset Scenario

Panel baseline for the 80% peak-power goal · Callsun 450 W two-pack

Use two 450 W panels per pack: 900 W DC nameplate, with a $760 per pack planning allowance ($380 per panel, about $0.84/W). This rounds the Amazon listing's $759.99 offer checked October 8, 2026. Panel reference.

This is the retail panel-cost baseline. Prices may change; taxes, delivery, inverter, batteries, mounting, wiring, permits and installation are additional. No extra bifacial gain is assumed. The advertised cell efficiency is not a multiplier on the 450 W rating.

The peak-power goal is to reduce grid demand to 20% of its measured peak. Required offset power = measured peak kW × 0.80. Panel DC nameplate alone does not establish that offset: usable AC output depends on sunlight, losses and inverter capacity. Batteries or shifted loads are needed when the peak occurs outside sufficient solar production. Battery energy also depends on how long the peak lasts. The energy-cost scenario below uses kWh separately.

Scenario only, not solar sizing, a production forecast or guaranteed savings. Use generation for the same last-24-hour window as the recorded Main consumption below.
