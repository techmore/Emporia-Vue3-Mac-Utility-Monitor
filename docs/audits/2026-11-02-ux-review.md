# UI/UX review — 2026-11-02

Reviewed: web dashboard (Dashboard, Circuits, Trends, Reports, Settings, Logs, circuit
detail), first-run/empty/offline states, the native menu-bar popover (light and dark),
desktop 1024–1440 px and phone 375 px, and basic accessibility.

**Method.** No real account or home data. A fictional 16-circuit house (13 months of
hourly history, 14 days of minute readings, one unnamed CT) was generated into a scratch
`energy.db`, with a fake poller appending a reading every minute. A second, empty
database covered the first-run state. The popover was rendered offscreen from
`MenuPopover.swift` against the same demo server. Screenshots are in
[`ux-2026-11-02/`](ux-2026-11-02/).

## Not fixed — recommended follow-ups

1. **No dark mode in the web dashboard.** The page ignores `prefers-color-scheme`; the
   menu-bar popover follows the system but the WKWebView dashboard is always light olive
   with a dark hero. Many templates use raw palette steps (`--olive-300`) as text colours,
   so this needs a semantic-token pass first (text, muted, surface, plate), then a dark
   token set.
2. **Navigation and information architecture.** 11 top-level items mix the core energy
   pages with lab integrations (House Lab, Home 3D, Radon, Aqara, HVAC, Kasa). Circuits
   and Logs live in a "Settings Workspace" (the Settings tab lights up while viewing
   Circuits), Settings and the workspace pages have two different sidebars, and the
   Action Center / Operational Summary blocks repeat on Dashboard, Circuits and Trends.
   Suggest: Dashboard · Circuits · Trends · Reports · Settings, with integrations under
   one "Home" menu.
3. **Trends page weight and keyboard flow.** ~1 MB of HTML; each heatmap cell has
   `tabindex="0"`, so keyboard users tab through ~5,400 cells. Prefer one focusable grid
   with arrow-key navigation plus the existing per-circuit summary table.
   *Keyboard part fixed 2026-11-02:* each heatmap is now one Tab stop (`role="grid"`,
   roving tabindex, arrow keys plus Home/End), and typical-week cells have spoken names.
   Page weight is unchanged.
4. **Unstyled blocks on Trends.** The typical-week table, whole-panel sentence and hourly
   solar scenario form use browser-default inputs, button and table spacing.
5. **Weather location is hard-coded** (latitude/longitude in `web.py` `/api/weather`).
   Make it a setting (and keep coordinates out of a public repository).
6. **Number precision is inconsistent:** "1223.0 W" in the hero, "1223 W" on the panel,
   kWh at 2–4 decimals. Suggest whole watts and 1–2 decimal kWh in summaries.
7. **Alerts are colour only.** Budget overrun (125%) and breaker safety show in-page;
   there is no notification from the menu-bar app when the poller stops or the budget is
   crossed. The popover's red/amber slot outlines have no legend.
8. **Popover sizing.** Fixed 440×560 leaves ~40% empty with a 16-slot panel; size to
   content or use the space for the 24h strip.
9. **Circuit detail periods.** "Day" shows 8 daily bars (the first partial); charts
   there and on Trends lack y-axis unit labels; Chart.js bar colours are hard-coded
   `oklch()` rather than read from CSS tokens as AGENTS.md asks.
10. **Empty/unmonitored breaker slots** render at 35% opacity and are hard to read.
11. **Credentials.** Settings says credentials are stored in `settings.json`; the roadmap
    already plans Keychain storage.
